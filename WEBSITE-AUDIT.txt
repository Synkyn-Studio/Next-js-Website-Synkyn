--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 OVERALL VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The site looks premium. The dark cinematic style, the motion work (loader,
pinned zoom, mega menu, page transitions) and the copy ("Less Syn, More Kyn",
"AI Generated. Human Directed.") fit an AI film studio well. The technical
base is good too: pages are server-rendered, URLs are clean, redirects and
canonicals are set, and there is structured data.

The weak spots are not the design. They are:

  1. Speed. The home page makes every visitor wait about 4 seconds, and the
     site ships very heavy CSS and JS.
  2. Unfinished content that visitors can see: empty cards and a duplicated
     project on the Library page.
  3. Lead capture. The only way to get in touch is a mailto link, which does
     nothing for many desktop users.
  4. Trust and proof. There are no case studies, results, testimonials or
     process pages, and no privacy policy.
  5. Social sharing. Every page shares the plain logo as its preview image,
     and the image size it declares is wrong.

Fix section A first. It is small work with the biggest effect.


================================================================================
 A. CRITICAL: fix first (visible to visitors or losing leads)
================================================================================

A1. Empty placeholder cards on the Library page
    Where:  src/data/works.ts, WORKS_ROW, entries "work-row-2" and "work-row-3"
    What:   Both entries have an empty title, thumbnail and video URL, and they
            are live on /library now (confirmed in the page HTML). Visitors see
            two blank cards that do nothing when clicked.
    Fix:    Fill them with real projects, or remove them until you have some.

A2. RedBus appears twice on the Library page
    Where:  src/data/works.ts. "RedBus" is in WORKS (around line 77) and again
            in WORKS_ROW (around line 114), with the same four Vimeo videos.
    What:   A portfolio with a repeated project looks thin.
    Fix:    Keep one of them and put a different project in the other slot.

A3. The home loader blocks every visit for about 3.8 seconds
    Where:  src/behaviors/home/loader.ts (FILL_MS 2700 + HOLD_MS 300 +
            OPEN_MS 820)
    What:   The loader runs on a fixed timer. It never finishes early, and it
            plays again every time someone clicks "Home" or the logo. Nearly
            4 seconds of nothing is where visitors leave, especially people
            arriving from ads and on phones. It also delays when Google sees
            the main content (the LCP metric), which hurts rankings.
    Fix:    - Show it only on the first visit of a session (for example, a
              sessionStorage flag) and skip it on in-site navigation.
            - Shorten it to about 1.2 to 1.5 seconds, or end it as soon as
              the hero is ready.
            - Skip it entirely for users who have "reduce motion" turned on.

A4. The contact flow depends only on mailto links
    Where:  src/components/contact/*, footer, header "Connect" button
    What:   The FAQ even says "Why is there no contact form?". Clicking a
            mailto link does nothing, or opens an unconfigured Mail/Outlook
            app, for anyone who uses Gmail or Outlook in the browser. That is
            a large share of desktop visitors, and those leads are lost
            without you ever knowing.
    Fix:    Keep the one-click email, but also add:
            - A "Copy email" button (copy to clipboard, then show a toast).
            - A short brief form: name, company, email, budget range,
              timeline, project type. Formspree, Resend or a Next.js route
              handler works, and it needs no backend server.
            - A WhatsApp Business button. Many clients in India expect one.
            - A "Book a 15-min call" link (Cal.com or Calendly).
            Then track form submits and email clicks as conversions in GA4.

A5. Contact page and 404 page block pinch-zoom
    Where:  src/data/seo-meta.ts, the "notFound" and "contact" viewport
            ("maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no")
    What:   This is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4) and Lighthouse
            flags it. Visually impaired users can't zoom the contact details,
            which is the one page they most need to read.
    Fix:    Use "width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover"
            everywhere, like the other pages already do.

A6. No privacy policy, and analytics run without consent
    Where:  site-wide (GTM + GA4 in src/app/layout.tsx). The only mention of
            privacy is inside the Terms page.
    What:   The site collects analytics data. India's DPDP Act 2023 requires
            a privacy notice, and you list the US, UK and UAE as markets. UK
            and EU visitors are also covered by GDPR, which requires consent
            before analytics run.
    Fix:    Add a /privacy page and link it in the footer next to Terms. Add
            a light cookie-consent banner using Google Consent Mode v2.

A7. The social share image is wrong on every page
    Where:  src/data/seo-meta.ts (og:image and twitter:image) and
            src/data/structured-data.ts (logo width and height)
    What:   Every page uses /images/logo.png and declares it as 1200x630. The
            file is actually 900x294. Links shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn,
            X and Slack show a cropped or stretched logo instead of a
            cinematic frame, which is a big missed opportunity for a film
            studio.
    Fix:    Make a real 1200x630 JPG or WebP for each page, such as a still
            from the showreel with the logo and page title. Next.js can also
            generate these from an opengraph-image.tsx file. Correct the
            width and height values in the structured data as well.


================================================================================
 B. PERFORMANCE: speed and Core Web Vitals
================================================================================

B1. main.css is 575 KB and blocks rendering on every page
    Where:  public/assets/main.css (plus cursors.css 72 KB, navbar.css 26 KB
            and footer.css 13 KB)
    What:   This is a pre-built Tailwind/template stylesheet, and most of its
            20,000+ lines are rules the site never uses. The browser has to
            download and parse all of it before it shows anything. On a 4G
            phone that alone takes seconds.
    Fix:    - Run PurgeCSS on it (or move to Tailwind v4 with automatic
              content scanning). A typical result is 30 to 60 KB.
            - cursors.css is 72 KB because 17 custom cursors are embedded as
              base64. Cursors only apply on desktop, so load them after
              first paint or as small .svg files.
            - Remove the "@import 'cursors.css'" from main.css. @import
              chains add an extra round trip before first paint.

B2. Heavy JavaScript loads on every page
    Where:  src/lib/runtime/vendors.ts, public/vendor/*
    What:   GSAP 120 KB, ScrollTrigger 77 KB, MotionPath 36 KB, Lenis 24 KB,
            SplitText 13 KB, DrawSVG 6 KB and Springer 3 KB, all loaded on
            every page, including Terms and 404 which barely animate. Add
            GTM, gtag and the Vimeo player on top of that.
    Fix:    Load each plugin only on the pages that use it. For example,
            MotionPath and DrawSVG are probably only needed on one page.
            Terms and 404 may only need Lenis.

B3. The home hero video is a Vimeo iframe
    Where:  src/components/home/HeroSection.tsx (hero-bg-iframe, plus a
            second Vimeo iframe for the showreel thumbnail and a third in the
            modal)
    What:   A Vimeo "background" iframe loads the whole Vimeo player (hundreds
            of KB of JS, several domains) before the first frame plays. It is
            the slowest way to show a hero video, and it controls the LCP.
    Fix:    Self-host a short, compressed loop as <video autoplay muted loop
            playsinline poster=...> in MP4 (H.264) and WebM (AV1/VP9), about
            1 to 2 MB and 720p for mobile. Show hero-poster.webp immediately.
            Keep Vimeo only for the full showreel in the modal.

B4. Unused large files in /public
    Files:  public/images/noise.webp       6.98 MB  (not referenced anywhere)
            public/images/rocket1.svg      809 KB   (not referenced)
            public/images/rocket.svg       496 KB   (not referenced)
            public/images/thumbnails/Camel.png      (not referenced)
    What:   These are deployed but never loaded, so the site isn't slower.
            They do bloat the repo and the deploys, and anyone can fetch
            them by URL.
    Fix:    Delete them, or move them to a separate design-assets folder
            outside the repo.

B5. Images that can shrink a lot
    - Team photos (public/images/album/*.png) are 218 to 284 KB each as PNG.
      As WebP or AVIF at display size they'd be about 20 to 40 KB each.
    - logo.png is 176 KB, while logo.webp (58 KB) is already used in the UI.
      A logo should be an SVG or a WebP under about 10 KB.
    - PerfectGirl-SansSerif.otf is 96 KB as OTF. Convert it to WOFF2 (about
      40 to 50 KB) and subset it to the characters you use.
    - ns-img-485.png (270 KB) is only the fallback. Fine to keep, but it
      could also be about 60 KB.
    - Library and print thumbnails from ImageKit: add ?tr=w-800 (or the right
      display width) to every one. The navbar Zlade image dropped from
      550 KB to 15 KB this way.

B6. GTM and gtag are both loaded
    Where:  src/app/layout.tsx loads GTM-KLBKS7TM and gtag G-PST0K7RG3R.
    What:   If GA4 is also configured inside GTM, every page view is counted
            twice, which inflates traffic and halves the bounce rate. Either
            way it is two analytics scripts.
    Fix:    Pick one. Recommended: GTM only, with GA4 configured inside it.
            Check GA4 real-time reports to confirm there's one page_view per
            visit.

B7. Measure before and after
    Run PageSpeed Insights (mobile) on /, /library and /printalbum before
    fixing A3, B1, B2 and B3, then again after. Aim for LCP under 2.5 s,
    CLS under 0.1 and INP under 200 ms on mobile.


================================================================================
 C. SEO
================================================================================

C1. The structured data describes a site search that doesn't exist
    Where:  src/data/structured-data.ts, WebSite.potentialAction.SearchAction
            ("/library?q={search_term_string}")
    What:   The Library page doesn't read ?q=. Structured data that doesn't
            match what the site actually does can be ignored, or treated as
            misleading, by search engines.
    Fix:    Remove the SearchAction, or build real search or filtering on
            /library.

C2. The FAQ has no FAQ structured data
    Where:  src/components/contact/FaqSection.tsx
    Fix:    Add FAQPage JSON-LD with the same questions and answers. It is
            cheap, and it helps AI search engines and answer boxes pick up
            your answers.

C3. Portfolio videos have no VideoObject structured data
    Where:  src/data/works.ts. There is one VideoObject on Home, none for the
            Library.
    Fix:    Add a VideoObject for each Library work (name, description,
            thumbnailUrl, uploadDate, embedUrl). This is how videos appear in
            Google Video results, which matters a lot for a film studio.

C4. Clean up the old meta tags
    Where:  src/data/seo-meta.ts
    What:   revisit-after, rating, distribution, expires, language,
            geo.position, ICBM, X-UA-Compatible, msapplication-* and the
            duplicate <meta name="title">. Search engines ignore all of these;
            they only add weight to every page.
    Fix:    Remove them. Keep title, description, robots, canonical, OG,
            Twitter and theme-color.

C5. There is only one page per topic
    What:   You rank for what you have pages for. Right now there is one home
            page that tries to cover films, ads, print, previs and AI VFX.
            People search for things like "AI ad film production Bengaluru",
            "AI product video agency India" or "AI VFX studio".
    Fix:    Add one page per service (see D1) and one page per case study
            (see D2), each with its own title, description, H1 and video.

C6. The sitemap's lastModified is hardcoded
    Where:  src/app/sitemap.ts, LAST_MODIFIED = "2026-09-07" for every URL.
    Fix:    Set a real date per page, or the build date. Add the new service
            and case-study URLs as you create them.

C7. Library data is inconsistent
    Where:  src/data/works.ts
    - Tags are mixed: "Youtube" / "YouTube" / "Insta" / "Reels" / "Film".
    - Categories are mixed: "reels", "Dragon Glimpse", "NBK111 Glimpse",
      "Crayons", "Ad Film".
    - One title is lowercase: "skroman automation".
    - Skroman appears twice under two different names.
    Fix:    Use one fixed set of categories (Ad Film, Film / Trailer, Social
            Reel, Print & Key Art, AI VFX) and consistent Title Case names.
            It also makes the filters described in D5 possible.

C8. The team data is inconsistent
    Where:  src/data/team.ts
    What:   Pruthvij's role is "Gen AI artist", but the alt text says
            "Co-founder and Gen AI Artist". Pick one. The CEO and MD should
            probably be listed first.

C9. Search Console and Bing Webmaster setup
    - Make sure the property is https://www.synkynstudios.com/ (the root,
      with www), not /library. This is what broke the Bing robots.txt test
      earlier.
    - Submit sitemap.xml in both tools.
    - Create a Google Business Profile for "Synkyn Studios, Bengaluru" and
      link it from the site. Local searches ("AI studio Bengaluru") depend
      heavily on it.


================================================================================
 D. CONTENT AND CONVERSION: what to add
================================================================================

These are in order of how much business they bring.

D1. Service pages (one each)
    Films / Trailers, Ad Films & Commercials, Print & Digital Campaigns,
    Storyboards & Previs, AI VFX & Set Extension. These already exist as
    the 5 Pipeline cards on Home. Make each card link to its own page with:
    what it is, 2 or 3 example videos, the process, turnaround time, what the
    client provides, typical deliverables (formats and aspect ratios), and a
    CTA.

D2. Case studies (the biggest trust gap)
    Right now the Library shows videos only. Buyers, especially agencies and
    brand managers, want to know:
      Brief -> Challenge -> Approach (AI + human) -> Result -> Numbers
    Example: "RedBus: GenAI cleanups on 4 spots, delivered in 5 days instead
    of a reshoot." Even 3 or 4 good case studies will do more than 20
    videos. Give each one its own URL for SEO.

D3. Proof and trust signals
    - Client testimonials: a quote, name, role and company logo.
    - Numbers: projects delivered, average turnaround, countries served,
      views generated.
    - The "Visionary Brands" logo wall: only show logos you have permission
      to show, and link each one to its case study.
    - Awards, press or festival selections, if you have any. The founders'
      IMDb credits are already a strong signal, so mention them in the About
      copy, not only as links.

D4. A "How we work" / process section
    Brief -> Moodboard & Previs -> Generation -> Human direction & compositing
    -> Grading & Sound -> Delivery. Include timelines. It answers "how can AI
    be cinematic?", which is the main doubt a buyer has.

D5. Library improvements
    - Filters by category (see C7) and by aspect ratio (16:9 / 9:16).
    - A shareable URL for each work (already partly there with
      #work-nbk111). Use a real /library/[slug] page so each work can be
      indexed and shared.
    - A "Before / After" slider for AI VFX and cleanup work. It is the
      single most convincing format for VFX.

D6. Pricing signals
    You don't need exact prices, but "Projects from ₹X" or 3 tiers
    (Social Pack / Ad Film / Campaign) filters out bad leads and brings in
    good ones. At minimum, add a budget-range dropdown to the brief form
    (A4).

D7. An "AI transparency / ethics" section
    Brands worry about copyright, likeness and legal risk with AI content.
    A short page covering which tools you use, how you handle rights,
    likeness consent, model licensing and brand safety builds trust with
    enterprise clients. The Terms page already covers some of this; turn it
    into a readable, client-facing page.

D8. Newsletter / "Latest drops"
    A small signup in the footer (Beehiiv, Substack or Resend Audiences) for
    new work. It keeps agencies and brand managers coming back.

D9. Blog / insights (for SEO over time)
    Examples: "AI vs traditional ad shoots: cost and time", "How we made the
    NBK111 glimpse", "Prompting for period-accurate visuals". Publish 1 or 2
    a month. They rank for the searches your future clients make and show
    expertise.

D10. Careers / collaborate page
    "We're hiring Gen AI artists / editors / colourists". It shows the studio
    is growing and attracts talent.

D11. More social channels
    Only Instagram and LinkedIn are linked. Add YouTube (you already host
    trailers there), Vimeo and Behance. Add them to the structured data
    "sameAs" list as well.

D12. A floating WhatsApp / "Start a project" button on mobile
    A small persistent CTA in the bottom corner on phones. Most of your
    social traffic will be mobile.


================================================================================
 E. ACCESSIBILITY
================================================================================

E1. Pinch-zoom is blocked on Contact and 404. See A5.

E2. Reduced motion
    Some CSS already respects prefers-reduced-motion, but the home loader,
    Lenis smooth scroll, the pinned zoom, marquees and the hero video still
    run. For users who have it turned on: skip the loader, turn off Lenis and
    show static sections.

E3. Low-contrast text
    A lot of text uses white/60 or accent/60 on near-black, and hero
    sub-lines are as small as 13px on mobile. Check key text with a contrast
    checker. Body text needs a 4.5:1 ratio. Also check that the yellow
    "is-current" link colour is readable in light mode.

E4. Decorative images and duplicated marquee content
    Home has 29 images with alt="". That is correct for decorative images and
    duplicated marquee logos. Make sure the first copy of each brand logo has
    a real alt (the brand name), and that the duplicated marquee sets are
    hidden from screen readers with aria-hidden.

E5. Keyboard navigation in the mega menu
    The "Work" and "Company" triggers are href="#" links that open only on
    hover. Keyboard users can reach them, but Enter does nothing. Make them
    buttons with aria-expanded that open on Enter / Space and close on Esc,
    with focus moving into the panel.

E6. Custom cursors
    Hide or restore the normal cursor for keyboard and touch users, and
    never hide the text cursor inside inputs (this matters once the form
    from A4 exists).


================================================================================
 F. SECURITY AND TECHNICAL HYGIENE
================================================================================

F1. Security headers are missing
    Live check: only Strict-Transport-Security is present.
    Add these in next.config.ts headers():
      - X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
      - Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
      - X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN (or CSP frame-ancestors 'self')
      - Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()
      - Content-Security-Policy that allows your real sources: self,
        ik.imagekit.io, player.vimeo.com, *.vimeocdn.com, youtube,
        instagram, googletagmanager and google-analytics.
      - Add "; includeSubDomains; preload" to HSTS once you're sure.
    Check the result at securityheaders.com. It currently scores about a D.

F2. No linting, tests or CI
    package.json has no ESLint config, no lint script and no tests. Add:
      - eslint.config.mjs with next/core-web-vitals
      - a "lint" script, and a GitHub Action that runs typecheck + lint +
        build on every push
      - optionally, a Lighthouse CI budget so performance can't silently
        regress.

F3. The git history is unreadable
    Recent commits are all "updated" or "first commit". Use short,
    descriptive messages ("Library: remove duplicate RedBus card"). It
    makes rollbacks and reviews possible.

F4. Content lives in code
    Works, prints and team live in TypeScript files, so every portfolio
    update needs a developer and a deploy. When you start publishing case
    studies and a blog (D2, D9), move this content to a headless CMS such as
    Sanity, Contentful or Payload, or at least to MDX files, so the team can
    publish without touching code.

F5. Monitoring
    Add Vercel Analytics / Speed Insights (real-user Web Vitals) and an
    uptime monitor (for example UptimeRobot). Optionally add Sentry to catch
    JS errors in the heavy animation code on unusual devices.

F6. The copyright year is hardcoded
    "© 2026" appears in the hero, mobile menu and footer. Generate it from
    new Date().getFullYear() so it never goes stale.


================================================================================
 G. SMALL POLISH ITEMS
================================================================================

  - "Less Syn / More Kyn" is the most memorable line on the site but it's an
    H2, while the H1 is a 13px sentence. That's fine for SEO, but make sure
    the visual hierarchy reads well on mobile.
  - Contact page: "Karnataka 560001". If you have a real studio address, show
    it with a Google Maps link. It builds trust and helps local SEO. If you
    don't, remove the PIN code.
  - Operating hours say "Mon–Fri". Add the expected reply time ("We reply
    within 24 h").
  - The home page title and description say "without delays". Something
    more specific, like "in days, not weeks", is stronger and more
    believable.
  - The mobile menu has no "Services" or "Work" grouping, only a flat list.
    Once the service pages exist, group them.
  - Add a custom favicon for dark and light browser tabs (favicon.svg can use
    prefers-color-scheme).
  - The 404 page is noindex (good). Give it links to the Library and
    Contact, not only Home.
  - The print album lists big brands (Visa, Volkswagen, Yonex). Make sure
    each one was client-commissioned or is clearly labelled as spec /
    concept work, to avoid any trademark issues.
